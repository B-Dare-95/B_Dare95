**The repetitive parts of Revit, handled for you.**

Every project has the same slow, manual tasks. B-Dare95 takes care of them so you can focus on the model itself.

> 🧹 **Clean models**
>
> Fix warnings, duplicates and off-axis elements in bulk.

> 🔗 **Faster coordination**
>
> Inspect links, trace MEP systems and work through clash reports without leaving Revit.

> 📐 **Quicker documentation**
>
> Create callouts, align grids and titles, and dimension in seconds.

> 🧱 **Smarter modelling**
>
> Split, match and manage walls, floors, shafts, rooms and stairs in a few clicks.

## Get started

Quick to install. No admin rights needed.

> 📥 **Download the installer**
>
> 📌 PLACEHOLDER: attach B_Dare95_Installer 2.0.bat here

1. **Download** the installer.
2. **Run** it. If pyRevit isn't on your computer yet, the installer adds it for you.
3. **Reload pyRevit** (pyRevit tab → Reload), or restart Revit.

The **B-Dare95** tab appears on your ribbon, ready to use.

**What you need:** Revit 2024 or newer · pyRevit (installed for you if missing) · No admin rights

Prefer to install pyRevit yourself? Get the latest release from [pyRevit on GitHub](https://github.com/pyrevitlabs/pyRevit/releases).
